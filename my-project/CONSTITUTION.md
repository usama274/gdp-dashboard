# GDP Dashboard - Project Constitution

## Project Vision
Build a world-class Human Resource Development Management (HRDM) and Classification Competency Platform for the Pakistan Shipping Bureau that enables effective personnel development, competency tracking, and maritime classification standards compliance.

## Core Principles

### 1. **Compliance & Governance**
- Adhere to IACS (International Association of Classification Societies) standards for information transparency and maritime classification
- Implement role-based access control with statutory and regulatory requirements
- Maintain audit trails for all critical operations
- Support SOLAS (Safety of Life at Sea) compliance requirements

### 2. **Competency-Driven Development**
- Track and manage individual competencies through comprehensive matrices
- Implement structured development plans with measurable outcomes
- Enable continuous learning through MCQ-based assessments and digital certificates
- Support witness surveys and supervised assessments for practical competency verification

### 3. **User-Centric Design**
- Provide intuitive, role-based dashboards for different stakeholder groups
- Support multi-channel access (web, mobile-ready)
- Enable digital workflows with QR authorization and e-signatures
- Facilitate seamless file uploads and knowledge library access

### 4. **Operational Excellence**
- Implement risk-based job assignment engine
- Track KPI and utilization metrics
- Maintain comprehensive backup and export capabilities
- Support both cloud (Supabase) and local (SQLite) deployments

### 5. **Data Integrity & Security**
- Implement Row-Level Security (RLS) in database layer
- Support secure file storage with access controls
- Enable encrypted credential management
- Maintain data consistency across distributed environments

## Stakeholders
- **Pakistan Shipping Bureau (PSB)** - System owner and governance body
- **Ship Owners & Operators** - Information consumers and compliance partners
- **Technical Authorities** - Assessors and authorizers
- **Trainees & Personnel** - Development and competency tracking subjects
- **Admin Users** - System management and oversight

## Success Criteria
1. ✅ 100% IACS/SOLAS compliance coverage
2. ✅ Zero data loss or security breaches
3. ✅ <2 second average response time for all operations
4. ✅ Support for 10,000+ concurrent users
5. ✅ 99.9% system uptime
6. ✅ Full audit trail for regulatory inspections
7. ✅ Seamless cloud and local deployment options
