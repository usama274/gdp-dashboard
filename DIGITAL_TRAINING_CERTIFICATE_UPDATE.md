# Digital Training / Refresher Certificate Update

## Added
- Admin can upload both **digital signature** and **digital stamp/seal** for CEO, Trainer, Tutor/Mentor, QMR, Principal/Chief Reviewer and Management.
- Training completion/refresher certificates are generated automatically after a trainee passes the MCQ assessment.
- Certificate includes:
  - Trainee name
  - Role
  - Training name
  - Certificate generated date
  - Completion date
  - Refresher / validity due date based on training validity months
  - Assessment result and score
  - Trainer signature + stamp
  - Tutor/Mentor signature + stamp
  - CEO signature + stamp
  - QR verification link
- Certificate HTML is saved in `training_certificates` and also linked from the trainee's `training_records` row.
- QR verification page now verifies both authorization certificates and training completion certificates.

## Database Updates
New table:
- `training_certificates`

Updated tables:
- `digital_signatures.stamp_data_uri`
- `training_records.training_certificate_id`
- `training_records.training_certificate_html`
- `training_records.certificate_generated_on`
- `training_records.refresher_due_date`

## Admin Workflow
1. Login as Admin.
2. Open Admin Control Center.
3. Open Authorized Digital Signatures for Certificates.
4. Upload signature and stamp for:
   - CEO
   - Trainer
   - Tutor/Mentor
5. Set usage as `Training Completion Certificate` or `All Certificates`.
6. Save.

## Trainee Workflow
1. Trainee completes training and passes MCQ.
2. System generates the digital training/refresher certificate.
3. Trainee can view and download the certificate from the assigned training page.

## Notes
- Certificates are controlled records and trainees cannot edit them.
- If signatures/stamps are changed later, future certificates will use the latest active controlled signature/stamp.
- Previously generated certificates retain their stored HTML unless regenerated by admin workflow/custom code.
